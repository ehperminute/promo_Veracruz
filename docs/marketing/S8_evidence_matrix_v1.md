# S8 — Traveler-preference evidence matrix (working draft v1)

**Project:** Veracruz tourism campaign | **Prepared:** 12 October 2026

## Scope
This is an initial **external evidence review**, not a completed campaign, demand forecast, or proof of interest in Veracruz. It uses the exact eight binary experience categories in `destination_master.csv`. The candidates below are *hypotheses to test*, and inclusion in a category means evidence-coded presence in the project, not current bookability.

## External research consulted

**B1 — Booking.com Travel Predictions 2025.** Online survey conducted July–August 2024, 27,713 adults planning travel in the next 12–24 months, in 33 countries/territories (including Mexico, US, Canada, several European countries, Japan and others). Reports growing interest in authentic experiences, less familiar places, wellness/adventure and nature-based activities. **Limitation:** platform-commissioned international survey; trends are not country-specific demand for Veracruz. https://www.booking.com/articles/travelpredictions2025.html

**E1 — Expedia Group Traveler Value Index 2025.** Research with more than 11,000 consumers across 11 markets. Expedia reports 61% using social media for trip inspiration, 73% saying influencer recommendations affected travel booking decisions, and 58% expecting to be more price-conscious. **Limitation:** platform research about international travel habits, not response rates for Veracruz ads. https://www.expedia.com/newsroom/travel-priorities-reinvented-expedia-groups-2025-traveler-value-index-signals-a-shift-in-consumer-priorities/

**E2 — Expedia Group Advertising, Science of Wanderlust (October 2025).** Reports that 71% said video influenced a travel decision versus 24% for static imagery. **Limitation:** influence is self-reported; not a causal estimate of increased conversion from video or evidence for a specific Mexican destination. https://www.expedia.com/newsroom/expedia-group-advertising-travel-content-research/

## What we can and cannot infer

**Observed in outside surveys:** stated travel motivations and travel-content preferences across surveyed markets.

**Present in our project:** coded tourism experiences, official product descriptions, and S7 destination/period allocations; S5/S6 occupancy evidence is available only for measured hotel centers.

**Not established:** market-by-market demand for Veracruz; willingness to travel to each destination; campaign costs by channel; real bookings, conversion rates, or return on investment. No inference that missing destination demand means low demand.

## First practical S8 proposal (not yet a final allocation)

Start by examining **three candidate messages** rather than creating fabricated personas: (a) nature/adventure, (b) cultural heritage/local traditions, and (c) coastal/culinary experiences. For each, intersect the theme flags with the **actual S7 selected destinations and seasons**, check documented attractions and access, then select relevant origin markets based on transport/access and actual market evidence. Treat social-media video as a **testable channel/creative hypothesis** informed by E1/E2, not a guaranteed best-performing medium. Keep the existing S7 overall budgets unchanged until real channel-pricing assumptions have been documented.
