# Limitations

1. The destination master is an analytical integration, not a census of all tourism activity.
2. Lack of DataTur/INAH coverage is missing measurement, not evidence of low demand.
3. INAH demand is attraction-specific.
4. DENUE establishment counts do not measure ecological or social carrying capacity.
5. Clustering similarity does not prove geographic accessibility or route feasibility.
6. Product association rules describe the sampled official offer, not tourist movement.
7. The included ML evaluation depends on larger INAH time-series data not bundled here.
8. No synthetic campaign data are used as evidence about current tourists.
