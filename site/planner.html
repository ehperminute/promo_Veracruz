How the optimization works

Input: 55 destination profiles × four periods, hypothetical $5,000 per placement and one placement per destination. Decision: which destination-period placements to select within budget. Rewards: representing new region-period combinations, new experience-period combinations and documented tourism readiness. Caution: a penalty applies to observed high-occupancy periods where center-level data exist; unknown occupancy is not treated as low.

These numbers are the results of the S7 prototype. They do not establish that promotion would increase tourist arrivals. This dashboard is a visualization of stored outputs, and changing filters does not re-optimize the selection.
